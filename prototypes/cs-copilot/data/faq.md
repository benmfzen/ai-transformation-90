# NATURA Foods — Customer Service Knowledge Base (fictional)

## [shipping-times] How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery takes 2-4 business days within Germany and 4-7 business days for other EU countries. Orders placed before 14:00 ship the same day.

## [shipping-costs] What does shipping cost?
Shipping is free for orders above 49 EUR. Below that, we charge 4.90 EUR within Germany and 9.90 EUR for other EU countries.

## [order-tracking] How can I track my order?
Once your order ships, you receive a tracking link by email. You can also find the tracking number in your account under "My Orders".

## [returns-policy] How do I return a product?
Unopened products can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Start the return in your account under "My Orders" and you will receive a prepaid return label. Opened food items cannot be returned for hygiene reasons, but please contact us if something is wrong with the product.

## [refund-timing] When will I get my refund?
Refunds are issued within 5 business days after we receive your return. Depending on your payment method, it can take another 2-3 business days until the amount appears in your account.

## [damaged-item] My delivery arrived damaged, what now?
We are sorry about that! Please send us a photo of the damaged item and the packaging. We will send a free replacement or refund the item — no return needed for damaged food products.

## [cancel-order] Can I cancel my order?
Orders can be cancelled as long as they have not shipped. Check the status under "My Orders" — if it still says "processing", contact us immediately and we will cancel it.

## [change-address] Can I change my delivery address?
The delivery address can be changed as long as the order has not shipped. After that, we recommend redirecting the parcel via the carrier's website using your tracking number.

## [product-ingredients] Where do I find ingredient information?
Every product page lists the full ingredients, nutrition facts and origin. All our products are free from artificial flavors and preservatives.

## [allergen-info] How do you handle allergens?
Allergens are printed in bold in the ingredient list on every product page and package. Many products are produced in facilities that also process nuts, gluten and sesame — see the "may contain traces" note per product.

## [storage-instructions] How should I store the products?
Store dry products cool, dry and away from direct sunlight. After opening, we recommend an airtight container. Best-before dates are printed on each package.

## [bulk-discount] Do you offer bulk discounts?
Yes — most products offer a bundle price at 3 or more units, applied automatically in the cart. For business orders above 500 EUR, contact our B2B team.

## [payment-methods] Which payment methods do you accept?
We accept credit card, PayPal, Klarna (invoice and installments), Apple Pay, Google Pay and SEPA direct debit.

## [invoice-request] Where do I find my invoice?
Invoices are attached to your order confirmation email and available as PDF in your account under "My Orders".

## [subscription-pause] Can I pause my subscription?
Yes, subscriptions can be paused, skipped or cancelled anytime in your account under "Subscriptions" — up to 2 days before the next scheduled delivery.

## [loyalty-program] How does the loyalty program work?
You earn 1 point per EUR spent. 100 points equal a 5 EUR discount on your next order. Points are credited once the return period has passed.
